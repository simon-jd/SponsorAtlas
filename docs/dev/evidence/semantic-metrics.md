| Public metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `lca.case_count` | Latest unique `case_number`; exporter rejects duplicate/null grain. Unknown employer history is retained. |
| `lca.certified_rate` | Sum of upstream `is_certified` (Certified and Certified - Withdrawn) divided by every latest case, including denied/withdrawn. Empty denominator returns null. |
| `lca.wage_p50`, `lca.wage_p75`, `lca.wage_p90` | Exact `PERCENTILE_CONT` of non-null, already annualized `annual_wage`. Not weighted by worker positions. Null wages remain in case denominator. |
| `postings.count` | Active posting-day observations (`is_active`); unlisted/deleted observations contribute zero. Across dates this counts posting-days, not unique vacancies. |
| `wages.oews_median` | Official annual `A_MEDIAN` for exactly one SOC × published AREA_TYPE/AREA × survey year; null for zero/multiple estimates or a suppressed/top-coded annual median. `estimate_count`, `median_raw`, `wage_status` explain unavailability. |

Both LCA and postings expose `employer`, `soc_major`, `soc`, `metro`, `fiscal_year`. Employer means canonical identity; historical employer attributes are not backfilled from the current dimension. Missing identities/classification/MSA are explicit `UNKNOWN`. An LCA county maps to at most one metropolitan CBSA; micropolitan members are not relabeled as metros. Jobs retain their original UTC observation date and its federal fiscal year. OEWS exposes applicable SOC/major/metro dimensions plus exact `area`, `geography_level`, `occupation_level`, `survey_year`, and raw flags; `fiscal_year` is null and employer is inapplicable. National/state/nonmetropolitan rows are not expanded across counties.

For major occupational benchmarks, filter `occupation_level=major` and an exact area/year; this returns the published major row. Filtering only `soc_major` leaves multiple detailed/major estimates and therefore returns null rather than an average of medians. Survey years are not assumed strictly comparable or a fiscal-year growth series. Different fact grains are not joined.

Views `lca_metrics`, `posting_metrics`, `wage_metrics` execute these interfaces. LCA and postings have fiscal-year rollups containing additive counts and certified numerator/denominator only. Fiscal-year rates are recomputed from their sums. Exact wage quantiles always read the original distribution and never consume averaged quantiles. OEWS has no median rollup. This follows [Cube's exact percentile modeling](https://docs.cube.dev/recipes/data-modeling/percentiles) and [DuckDB aggregate semantics](https://duckdb.org/docs/stable/sql/functions/aggregates.html).
